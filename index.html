ΟΝΟΜΑΤΑ

onómata — “names”

A wind of forgetting has blown across Olympus, and the gods no longer remember what anything is called. You are the young messenger. Each of the ten shrines keeps ten things on pedestals — walk up to each one to learn its Greek name.

Then step up to the altar for the trial: the god calls out a name and you run to the right thing, and afterwards you name each one yourself. Relight all ten flames and you will know 100 Greek words.
